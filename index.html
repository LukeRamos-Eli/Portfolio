01 · About

The short version

A quick look at who I am and what drives the work.

Hey, I'm Luke - 3rd year Computer Engineering student and a developer who likes building software that replaces messy, real-world processes with something people can actually trust.

My two most recent builds say a lot about how I think: a Barangay Emergency Response System that gets help routed faster during emergencies, and a Smart Facility Booking platform that takes scheduling for schools off whiteboards and spreadsheets.

I work across the stack - from C# desktop applications to React and Tailwind on the front end - and I care as much about the workflow a tool replaces as the code that runs it.
